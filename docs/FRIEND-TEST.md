# Testing Lyric Stars: a quick guide for friends

Thanks for trying it! This takes about 15 minutes, plus however long you want to listen.

## Install

Follow `INSTALL.md`. When Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**. The app is free and isn't signed, so Windows doesn't recognise it yet.

**Please time this:** from opening the download link to the first star on screen. Tell me how long it took.

## Connect

- If I've added your Spotify email to the app, click **Connect Spotify**.
- If I haven't, or Spotify says you're not allowed, click **Use without logging in**. It reads the Spotify app on your PC directly.

## Things to try

Play a few songs you know well, in whatever languages you listen to, and try each of these:

1. **Timing.** Do words light up when they're sung? If a song is early or late, press `[` or `]` to nudge it. Does it remember your nudge next time that song plays?
2. **Pause, skip, and jump around in a song.** The stars should catch up within a second.
3. **Display styles.** Press `F` for fullscreen and again to come back to the window.
4. **Settings** (`S`). Change the theme, turn on album colours, and switch between line and word mode (`M`).
5. **Leave it running** through a long playlist. Does anything freeze, slow down, or log you out?

## Something wrong?

Wrong lyrics, words out of time, a crash, a confusing message — anything:

1. Press `D` while the problem is on screen.
2. Click **Copy report**.
3. Paste it into a message to me, with a sentence about what you saw.

The report includes the song, the app's timing numbers, and recent errors. It doesn't include your Spotify login or password, and the app never sends it anywhere by itself.

**Wrong-song lyrics matter most.** If you see lyrics from a different song, please report it even if it happened only once.

## Updates

When I fix things, the app tells you that a new version is out (Settings → Desktop app). Download the new installer and run it over the old one. Your settings are kept.
